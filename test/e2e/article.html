The rise of solar power

Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic cells. Dr. Smith said the cost fell by 90% since 2010. This is remarkable[1].

Prices keep dropping every year, and manufacturers expect that trend to continue. Many countries now add more solar capacity than any other source of power. Storage costs are also falling quickly, which helps the grid.

  • Cheaper panels drive adoption worldwide.
  • Batteries make solar available at night.

Critics point to land use and recycling. Researchers are working on both problems at once, with promising early results from several laboratories around the world.

In the long run solar energy could supply a large share of global electricity demand, according to many independent analysts and agencies.
