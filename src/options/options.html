Open any article and click the Hearline icon (or press Alt+H). Tip: the HQ voices below sound much more natural than your system voices.

HQ voices (offline)

Natural-sounding voices that run entirely on your device — nothing you read is ever sent anywhere. English only (US & UK). One-time download of about 90 MB, then it works offline.

After installing, open the voice menu in the player to choose a voice. The first 6 voices are included; the others download (~0.5 MB each) when you first pick them, or use “Download all voices”.

No voices on Linux?

Chrome uses the system speech service. On Ubuntu run sudo apt install speech-dispatcher espeak-ng, then restart Chrome. Or install the HQ voices above — they don’t need anything else.

Privacy

Hearline has no account, no analytics, and no servers. Page text is only ever spoken by your browser or by the on-device HQ voices. The only network request is the optional one-time voice download from Hugging Face when you press “Install HQ voices”.
